🧭 How to Read Your Running Report

Welcome to your personalized running summary! Here's a quick guide to help you understand and enjoy your progress:

  • 🏷️ Title & Adjective: Your unique runner identity is highlighted at the top — based on your consistency, distance, and pace.
  • 📊 Quick Stats: Just below your name, you’ll find:
    • Total runs completed
    • Total distance covered
    • Your longest weekly streak (with at least one run every week!)
  • 📌 Milestone Table: See your key runs:
    • First Run: Your starting point — distance, pace, and time.
    • Longest Run: The farthest you've gone in one go.
    • Fastest Run: Your quickest pace (only runs above 1 km counted).
  • 💬 Reflection Paragraph: A motivational note generated just for you — celebrating growth and cheering you on for what’s ahead.
  • 📈 Consistency Graph: A visual summary of your run distances over time. Spot your patterns and trends.
  • ⬇️ Download Your Report: Tap or long-press in the yellow space at the bottom of the report download and share your progress. Great for staying motivated or showing it off to friends!
